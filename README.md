💡 GardiaFit: Your Proactive Health Companion

GardiaFit is a mobile health application concept aimed at promoting proactive health management and providing targeted support for specific demographics. Our core mission is to empower users through awareness and guidance, focusing on cognitive health, vitality, and well-being.

🎯 Core Focus Areas

GardiaFit is designed to be a supportive tool, initially focusing on two key areas:

1. Cognitive Health and Elderly Support

Target: Seniors and individuals concerned about cognitive decline or memory issues (often associated with diseases like Alzheimer's or dementia).

Goal: To provide simple, engaging tools or resources that aid in cognitive exercise, tracking patterns, and encouraging habits that support brain health and reduce the instances of forgetfulness.

2. Women's and Girls' Wellness

Target: Women and girls across various life stages.

Goal: To offer resources, tracking, and information related to menstrual health, reproductive well-being, and overall vitality, recognizing the unique physiological needs of this group.

🚧 Current Status: Raw Code and Development in Progress

GardiaFit is currently in its early development stage. The repository contains raw code, proofs-of-concept, and initial structure for the mobile application and cloud integration.

This is not yet a fully functional application.

The code is subject to frequent and significant changes as the architecture is finalized.
